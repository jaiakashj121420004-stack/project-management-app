## Full description (both stores, ≤4000 chars)
Jotspring is a calm creative workspace for your projects, ideas and daily to-dos.

Plan your work on kanban boards with columns, cards, checklists, due dates and goals. Write in a rich block editor with headings, task lists, tables, formulas, images and embeds. Sketch ideas on a freeform canvas with a pen, highlighter, shapes, sticky notes and arrows. See everything on a calendar, and keep a simple daily to-do list for the small stuff.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Kanban boards — drag cards between columns, add checklists, labels, priorities and due dates
• Rich notes — a full block editor with headings, lists, tables, math and media
• Canvas — an infinite whiteboard for brainstorms, diagrams and mind maps
• Calendar — month, week, day and timeline views of every due date
• To-do lists — daily and weekly lists, with repeating tasks
• Collaboration — share a board and work together in real time
• Reminders — due-date reminders by notification and email
• Your look — day and night themes, custom colours and fonts

FREE AND PRO
Jotspring is free to use with up to 3 project boards. Jotspring Pro unlocks unlimited boards, the whiteboard canvas, custom colours, email reminders and more. Team plans cover a whole class or studio.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Pro and Team are auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly or yearly). Payment is charged to your Apple ID / Google Play account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your account settings after purchase.
